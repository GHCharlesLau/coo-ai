# COO AI requirements audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-13  
**Sources:** `references/Building COO AI Agent.docx` is the requirements checklist. `references/Jamie_Final_SBHK Campaign Proposal_HK2A.pdf` is visual inspiration only. The PDF's claims, campaign copy, logos, and photographs are not COO AI source material and are not reused.

## Summary

New campaigns use requirements version 3. It adds explicit intake for blacklisted terms; structured ROPES work including stakeholder analysis; media tier and KOL assessment; stronger FAQ, KPI, and evaluation gates; and an HTML-first deck engine paired with a native editable PowerPoint renderer (real text, shapes, tables, charts, and picture frames; no speaker notes). Older records that predate this version load with requirements version 2 and retain their prior gate behavior. Legacy-format runs remain supported. Four stored campaigns and their 21 present JSON artifacts parsed successfully with the current models; no stored campaign artifacts were modified.

Status meanings: **Covered** means represented in a typed artifact and checked by a deterministic gate or renderer test. **Partial** means the agent provides a safe workflow and records uncertainty, but a client or qualified reviewer still owns an important decision. **Missing** means no current implementation was found.

## PDF design audit

The reusable design cues are its warm off-white canvas, strong display typography, sparse accent rules, alternating split-image/editorial compositions, dark section dividers, and diagrams that explain relationships or sequence. COO AI's default palette uses warm neutral, graphite, and coral; a per-campaign `palette` in `slides.json` overrides it for the deck, DOCX, and PDF. `template_path` is ignored. PowerPoint slides are native and editable, with real text, shapes, tables, charts, and picture frames rather than screenshots. Supplied or licensed images require source/usage/alt-text metadata, which appears in the slide and image descriptions and is never hidden in speaker notes.

The reference PDF also contains crowded text-heavy pages and campaign-specific material. COO AI does not copy its logos, suicide-prevention claims, campaign lines, or photographs. Diagrams remain the default when the user has no cleared image asset.

Renderer coverage is in `tests/test_renderer.py` and `tests/test_renderer_quality.py`: diagrams/charts, layout variety, theme and fonts, subtitle/body fields, image placement and rights metadata, citations, missing/unsafe assets, long text, overflow, overlap, and slide bounds. PPTX export needs no LibreOffice or PowerPoint; the deck gate checks 16:9 geometry, editable text per slide, slide count, no speaker notes, and no full-slide screenshots.
